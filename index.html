About me

I'm a full stack developer at Jisap, a business group based in Lorca, Spain. My day-to-day goes from the ERP core to the screens people use: Microsoft Dynamics AX and X++, .NET APIs, SQL Server, and Angular and Ionic front ends.

Outside work I love making games travel from a PC to any screen with as little latency as possible. That curiosity turned into a set of open-source projects around Sunshine, Moonlight and WebRTC: from a C++ streaming host to a Samsung TV app and a browser client with a WebAssembly core.

I enjoy the whole stack, from encoder pipelines and network protocols to the last pixel of a user interface.

Moonlight WebRTC Web

          
Play in any desktop browser. Video goes through the browser's hardware WebRTC pipeline, while a 25 KB WebAssembly core handles authentication and gamepad input.

          
    
            
  • Zero dependencies, nothing to install where you play
    • 
            
  • Gamepad polling every 4 ms with no garbage collection pauses
    • 
            
  • Picks only the codecs the browser can decode
